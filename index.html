Why raw? Your dog will show you.

Raw meals are what a dog's body is built for: muscle meat, organs, ground bone and a little fruit and veg. We make every batch by hand in our Portland kitchen, freeze it within hours and bring it to your door. Many owners notice the difference within a few weeks.

  • More energy, longer walks

    Many owners notice more bounce on walks within a few weeks.

  • Smaller, firmer stools

    No fillers means less waste, and it is the first thing most owners spot.

  • A coat that shines

    Many owners notice a softer, glossier coat after a month or two.

  • Fewer itchy days

    One protein per recipe makes it easier to avoid what your dog reacts to.

Questions? We have answers.

Switching food is a big step for you and your pet. Here is what people ask us most. If your question is not here, write to the kitchen and a real person answers.

Raw feeding means meals made from raw muscle meat, organ meat, ground bone, vegetables and a little fruit, instead of cooked kibble. Our recipes are complete meals: you thaw a pack and serve it, nothing to add.

Most dogs switch over 7 to 10 days. Start with a quarter of the meal raw and the rest the old food, and raise the raw part every two or three days. Keep the portion size the same and slow down if stools get soft. Cats often take longer: mix in a spoonful at a time.

Keep packs in the freezer at −18 °C for up to six months. Thaw one in the fridge overnight and use it within three days. Do not thaw in the microwave or in hot water, and do not refreeze a thawed pack.

Most adult dogs eat 2 to 3 % of their body weight a day; puppies and kittens eat more. The meal finder above gives you a starting point. Weigh your pet every two weeks and adjust by about 10 %, and ask your vet if your pet has a health condition.

We deliver around Portland on Tuesdays and Fridays in insulated boxes with ice packs, or you can pick up at the kitchen on Dock Road. Send an order request and we confirm the day and the total by email or text. You pay on delivery or pick-up.

Handle it like the raw meat you cook for yourself: wash hands, bowls and surfaces with hot soapy water after serving, and keep raw food away from small children. Every batch is frozen within hours of making and stays frozen until it reaches you.
